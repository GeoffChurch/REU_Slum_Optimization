What to look for

  • Raw flow piles up where everyone upstream passes through. "Against no buildings" compares each cell's share of all walking with its share when the same homes walk to the same edge across open ground, so what remains is where the buildings channel the flow: the corridors, throughout the block. Nothing is floored, so the watershed in the middle, where the open-field flow nearly vanishes, glows as a knot.
  • "In standard errors" divides that log ratio by its standard error, reading each cell's flow as a count of walkers (Monroe, Colaresi and Quinn's z-score). The knot is a ratio of two tiny counts, so it fades without any clamp; large corridors gain, because more walkers make the same ratio surer.
  • Under the uniform metric a channel conducts in proportion to its width, so the clearings are scattered openings. The flow they redirect still runs in long corridors through existing lanes, most visibly at the east seam ("Change in flow").
  • Under the sightline metric long straight runs conduct better, and the flow gathers into lanes running to the north edge, which the sightline greedy extends.
  • Each metric's own optimizer scores best on it, but SIMP's clearing comes within 0.004 of the sightline greedy on the sightline metric, while the sightline greedy scores lowest on the uniform one.

Lens A is 1 − (J/J₀)1/2 for the block against itself uncleared. Escape time is each open cell's expected time to reach the street; cells a clearing newly opens are left blank on that layer.
